Where Did I Put It?

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

1. Introduction

Where Did I Put It? is an Android application designed to help users remember where they have stored their belongings.

We respect your privacy. The application is designed to work offline and does not require an account.

2. Information Stored

The application allows you to record item names, storage locations, rooms and photographs of belongings.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not automatically uploaded to servers operated by us.

3. Photographs

You may choose photographs using your device's photo picker.

Selected photographs are copied into the application's private storage. The application does not require broad access to your photo library.

4. Backups and Restoring Data

You can manually export and import inventory backups, including complete ZIP backups containing photographs.

You control where exported backup files are saved and whether they are shared with other applications or cloud services. The privacy practices of those services are governed by their own policies.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

The application does not require an internet connection for its core features.

It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting services or online user accounts.

6. Data Sharing

We do not automatically transmit your saved belongings or photographs to third parties.

Information may leave your device when you choose to export, share or upload a backup.

7. Data Deletion

You can edit and delete belongings within the application.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its application data normally removes locally stored inventory information and application-managed photographs.

Exported backup files are separate and must be deleted from their saved locations if no longer required.

8. Security

Inventory information is stored using Android application storage mechanisms. Users should protect their devices and exported backup files against unauthorised access.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed towards children. It does not operate user accounts or knowingly collect children's information through developer servers.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this webpage.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact: appwheredidiputit@gmail.com
